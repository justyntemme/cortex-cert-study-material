## Confirmed outside the blueprint

- The September 2026 handbook gives a passing **scaled score of 860 on a 300–1000 scale** for all Palo Alto Networks certification exams. This is not an 86% raw-correct requirement. [Certification Handbook, “What Is a Scaled Score?” and “Why Use Scaled Scoring?”, page 5](https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/content/dam/pan/en_US/assets/pdf/ebooks/panw-certification-handbook.pdf).
- The handbook specifies a 15-day wait after the first failed attempt, 30 days after the second, and 90 days after the third and subsequent failures. Allow for the applicable interval when planning against a work deadline. [Certification Handbook, “Exam Retake Policy,” page 6](https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/content/dam/pan/en_US/assets/pdf/ebooks/panw-certification-handbook.pdf).
- The September 2026 FAQ lists Cloud Security Engineer among the $250 specialist exams, with possible additional taxes and fees. [Certification Program FAQ, exam-cost table, page 2](https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/content/dam/pan/en_US/assets/pdf/datasheets/education/certification-faq.pdf).
- Pearson describes the program's assessments as using multiple-choice, matching, and ordering questions. This general statement does not establish the exact mix for Cloud Security Engineer. Pearson also distinguishes total appointment time from time spent answering exam questions. [Pearson program page, “How To Register” and “Exams Preparation”](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/paloaltonetworks.html).
